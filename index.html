🌍 Open Source World

A fully-featured open multiplayer world with banks, jobs, crypto, weed system, hunger/thirst, and more.

🚀 Server Status: Online
🌟 Open Source World – Coming Soon to a Server Near You

✨ Key Features

🏦 Bank System

Full economy with ATM, transfers, transaction history, and bank cards. Persistence across sessions.

🚕 Jobs

Taxi and Gas delivery jobs with XP and cash rewards. Realistic NPC interactions.

₿ Crypto Lab

Mine SWC coins and exchange them for real money. Persistent mining progress with terminal UI.

🌿 Weed System

Plant, grow, harvest and sell weed. Inventory-based with persistent data.

🍔 Hunger & Thirst

Realistic survival mechanics with restaurants and vending machines. Don't starve!

🎮 Customization

Change your skin, equip back weapons, and choose your own name color (persistent).
